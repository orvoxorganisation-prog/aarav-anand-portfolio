About Aarav Anand

Commerce student from Mumbai. Risk-curious by default. I combine finance, technology and operations to turn uncertainty into clear, defensible decisions.

My work revolves around understanding risk rather than avoiding it.

I'm Aarav Anand, a Grade 11 commerce student from Mumbai with a growing interest in financial risk management, portfolio analysis, data analytics and entrepreneurship. I've built financial models, credit-risk analyses, portfolio-optimization systems, equity research reports and retail data-analytics projects — each one sharpening how I reason about uncertainty, return and the trade-offs between them.

Alongside finance, I enjoy using technology to improve processes and build products. As a Founding Member, Chief Operating Officer and Chief Risk Officer at ORVOX Youth Forum, I design operational systems, manage organizational workflows, oversee risk and build the technology infrastructure behind nationwide student initiatives.

Long-term, I'm aiming for a career in Enterprise Risk Management, Financial Risk Management and strategic decision-making — while continuously learning about AI, data and finance. Outside the spreadsheets, you'll find me training calisthenics, reading about markets, experimenting with new ideas and building side projects.
