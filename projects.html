Multi-Asset Portfolio Risk & Performance Analysis

A five-asset global portfolio (Aug 2020 – Sep 2024) spanning Indian equities, US technology and precious metals — Nifty MidSmallCap 400, Nifty Alpha Low-Vol, Nasdaq 100, Gold/GLD and Silver at 40/25/20/10/5 weights — evaluated end-to-end for growth, risk and tail behavior.

18.40%CAGR
1.07Sharpe ratio
11.90%Volatility
−20.93%Max drawdown
Microsoft ExcelHTML ReportValue at RiskMonte CarloCorrelation
Case study

My role

Sole analyst — designed the methodology, built the Excel analytics and authored a professional HTML research report.

What it covers

  • Portfolio growth, CAGR, annualized volatility and Sharpe ratio
  • Maximum drawdown, Value at Risk (VaR) and Monte Carlo simulation outcomes
  • Cross-asset correlation and quantified diversification benefits

Key findings

  • Portfolio-level volatility came in below its individual components — a real diversification benefit.
  • Surfaced concentration risk: roughly 65% India-equity exposure.
  • Flagged weak risk-adjusted performance from Silver and underestimated tail risk in simulations.
